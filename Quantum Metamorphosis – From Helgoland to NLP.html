Page 3 · Matrices: Order Matters (A×B ≠ B×A)

With ordinary numbers, 3×5 = 5×3. In quantum mechanics, operations are matrices, and changing their order changes the answer. This was Heisenberg's discovery that night.

A = X gate (0↔1 flip): [[0,1],[1,0]]
B = Z gate (phase flip): [[1,0],[0,−1]]
Start vector v = (1, 0)

Remember: the order of quantum gates changes the result of a circuit. That is why the gate sequence in QFT and Grover is written so carefully.

Page 4 · Superposition and Measurement

A qubit can be a mix of 0 and 1. When we measure it, we get only one answer, and the probability is decided by the angle.

Real life: a coin spinning in the air is a mix of heads and tails. The moment it lands on your hand, you get one result. In quantum mechanics this mix is real, not just a lack of knowledge.

Page 5 · Rovelli's Idea: Everything Is Relations

Rovelli (Relational Quantum Mechanics) says an electron has no property on its own. A property appears only when it interacts with something else.



Real life: you are different with a friend, different with a teacher, different alone. All three are real. A person, too, is known through relations.

Page 6 · NLP: Tokens and Padding

In NLP (Natural Language Processing), text must be converted into numbers for the computer. First, a sentence is split into tokens (words). The model needs all sentences to have the same length, so short sentences are filled with PAD tokens.

Max length: 5
The mask for PAD is 0: it tells the model this slot is empty, so ignore it. A longer sentence gets cut off (truncated).

Page 7 · Word Vector Embeddings
